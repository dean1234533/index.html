Frequently Asked Questions

What areas does DB's Workouts cover?+

DB's Workouts provides outdoor personal training across East London and surrounding areas, with 126 dedicated area pages. Dean covers everywhere within about 5.5 miles of Stratford, including Whitechapel, Shoreditch, Bethnal Green, Mile End, Hackney, Islington, Greenwich, Bermondsey, Tottenham and Canary Wharf, plus wider Essex towns such as Ilford, Romford, Barking and Loughton.

Does Dean travel to me for personal training?+

Yes. Dean trains clients outdoors in local parks and green spaces, and can also train you in your own home garden. Choose your nearest area page, then book a free consultation to agree a convenient location.

How much does a personal trainer cost in East London?+

A single session is £45 (pay as you go). Blocks are cheaper per session: 5 sessions for £214, 10 sessions for £406 or 15 sessions for £576 (£38.40 per session). Pair sessions are for two people and cost 1.5 times the corresponding one-person price. Pay as you go is £67.50 total for both people per session.

How do I book a personal trainer?+
