Why Train Outdoors?

Outdoor training gives you fresh air, natural light and a constantly changing environment — all of which improve mood, energy, and consistency. Park sessions combine resistance training, bodyweight exercises and cardio without the need for a gym. Every session is structured, safe and tailored to your goals.

What to Expect

  • Fully personalised sessions — goals, fitness level, and any injuries all considered
  • Combination of strength, bodyweight, HIIT and mobility work
  • All equipment provided — no need to buy anything
  • Sessions at a time and park that works for you
  • Ongoing support between sessions and nutrition guidance
  • Progress tracked and programme updated as you improve
