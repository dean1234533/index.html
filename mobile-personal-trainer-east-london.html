Personal training that comes to you

Mobile outdoor personal training means choosing a convenient location with Dean, then following a programme built around your goals. Sessions can take place in a local park or suitable home garden. Share your postcode during the free consultation so you can agree a practical venue and check availability before booking.

Local park or home garden?

A park gives you room for circuits, cardio and portable strength equipment. A suitable home garden offers another outdoor option closer to your door. Training around work and family life

Early mornings, evenings and weekends can be discussed around your availability. You do not need to organise equipment or pay a gym membership. Each 1-to-1 session includes a warm-up, coached exercises and a cool-down, with the programme adjusted to your level. Confirm your preferred time and location during the consultation.

Strength, fitness and boxing padwork

Your mobile PT programme can include strength work, cardio circuits, boxing fitness or a mix of exercises. Dean adapts the training to what you want to achieve. Blocks of 5, 10 or 15 sessions also include personalised nutrition and workout plans in the DB's Workouts app to support your routine between sessions.

Your questions answered

Which areas does your mobile personal trainer cover?+

DB's Workouts covers East London, including Ilford, Goodmayes, Stratford, Wanstead, Walthamstow, Romford, Barking and nearby areas. Send your postcode to Dean to confirm a venue and availability.

Can you train me in my home garden?+

Yes, a suitable home garden is an outdoor training option. Discuss the available space with Dean during your consultation.

Do I need a gym membership or my own equipment?+

No. Dean provides portable training equipment for your outdoor session. A gym membership is not required.

How much does mobile personal training cost?+

Pay as you go is £45 per session. Blocks are £214 for 5, £406 for 10 or £576 for 15 sessions. Confirm your location and availability before booking.
