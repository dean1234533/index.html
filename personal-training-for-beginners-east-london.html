You can start from where you are

You do not need to be fit, confident or experienced before your first session. Dean begins with a conversation about your goals, fitness history and any limitations you want him to understand. An assessment helps shape the programme, so the exercises and pace reflect your starting point rather than someone else's routine.

What your first personal training session involves

Your session includes a warm-up, a main workout adapted to your level and a cool-down. Dean explains the movements and coaches you through them. Exercises can use bodyweight, resistance bands and portable kit. As you learn the movements, the programme can build towards more strength, stamina and confidence.

Your questions answered

Do I need to get fit before booking a personal trainer?+

No. Dean builds sessions around your starting ability. Beginners and people returning after a break are welcome.

What should I bring to an outdoor PT session?+

Wear comfortable training clothes and suitable footwear, and bring water. Dean provides the session equipment.

Can I try a session without buying a block?+

Yes. Pay-as-you-go personal training is £45 per session. You can start there without a contract or minimum commitment.

What happens in the free consultation?+

It is a 20-minute conversation, in person or by phone, about your goals, fitness history and whether Dean's outdoor personal training is a good fit.
