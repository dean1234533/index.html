Quick answer

Online personal training gives you the same one-to-one attention as training in person, from wherever suits you. Each 45-minute session is just you and Dean, with exercises and pace chosen for your goals and fitness level. It is personal coaching, not a group class.

How online personal training works

Start with a free consultation. Dean talks through your goals, your fitness history and any limitations. He then sets you up in the DB's Workouts app, where your personalised workout and nutrition plan live, and sends you a personal access link by email. From there you train in 45-minute sessions and check in regularly, so your plan keeps adapting as you progress.

Built around the equipment you have

You do not need a gym membership. Tell Dean what you can access, whether that is a full gym, a few dumbbells, resistance bands or just your bodyweight, and the training is planned around it. Online training also suits busy schedules, travel and anyone who lives outside East London.

Your workout and nutrition plan in the app

Every online client is set up in the DB's Workouts app with a workout plan and a nutrition plan written for them. It is the place to follow your programme between sessions, with Dean checking in on how it is going. The app is where your plans live. The coaching itself comes from Dean.

Your questions answered

How much does online personal training cost?+

Online 1-to-1 personal training costs £30 per 45-minute session. Your workout and nutrition plan in the DB's Workouts app and your regular check-ins are included.

Is online training a class or one-to-one?+

It is one-to-one. Every session is just you and Dean, so the session is built around your goals, your fitness level and the equipment you have, with no group classes.

What is included with online personal training?+

You get 45-minute 1-to-1 online sessions with Dean Burt, a personalised workout plan and nutrition plan in the DB's Workouts app, and regular check-ins to keep you on track.

How do I get my workout and nutrition plan?+

Dean sets you up in the DB's Workouts app and sends you a personal access link by email. Your workout and nutrition plan live there, so you can follow them between sessions.

Do I need a gym membership or equipment?+

No gym membership is required. Tell Dean what equipment you have access to, whether that is a full gym, a few dumbbells or just your bodyweight, and he will build the plan around it.

Can beginners do online personal training?+

Yes. Dean starts with a free consultation to understand your goals, fitness history and any limitations, then builds the training at a level that works for you.

Do you guarantee results?+

No. Results depend on your starting point, consistency and habits between sessions. Dean helps you set realistic goals and tracks your progress with regular check-ins.

How do I book online personal training?+

Book a free consultation through the contact page or message Dean on WhatsApp on 07752 300937. You can talk through your goals before you start.
