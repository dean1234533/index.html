My Story

Why I Became a Personal Trainer

I know what it feels like to be out of shape, unmotivated, and not sure where to start. I've been there. That frustration — and the life-changing difference that great coaching made for me — is exactly why I dedicated my career to transforming other people's health.

I started coaching outdoors in East London parks because I believe fitness should be accessible, energising, and free from the intimidating atmosphere of a commercial gym. The fresh air, the green space, the community — there's nothing like it.

Nine years in, I still feel the same excitement every single session. Seeing someone hit a goal they thought was impossible — that never gets old.

  • Outdoor-first approach for maximum results and motivation
  • Every session tailored to your goal — no generic programmes
  • Accountability and support beyond just your training hours
  • Real results, no gimmicks, no shortcuts
Training Philosophy

The Way I Coach

My approach is built on principles that have consistently produced lasting results for hundreds of clients — not trends, not quick fixes.

🎯

Precision Programming

Every session is designed around your specific body, your specific goals, and your specific lifestyle. No two clients train the same way.

🌿

Outdoor Environment

Training outdoors dramatically improves energy, mood, and consistency. Nature is the best gym — and it's free.

🤝

Holistic Support

I'm not just your trainer for an hour. I support you with nutrition, accountability check-ins, and mindset coaching around the clock.

📈

Progressive Overload

Every block of training progressively challenges your body so you never plateau. Consistent progress is built into every plan.

🧠

Mindset & Habit

Physical transformation starts in the mind. I coach mental resilience and healthy habits alongside the physical — because that's what makes results last.

🏆

Long-Term Thinking

I don't care about quick fixes. I care about building fitness that becomes part of your identity — something you carry for life.

The Journey

9 Years. One Mission.

From first qualification to building a thriving PT business — here's how DB's Workouts was built.

🎓
2016
REPs Certified — First Clients

Gained my Level 3 Personal Training qualification and took on my first outdoor clients in Walthamstow's Lloyd Park. Poured everything into those early sessions.

🌿
2017–2018
Outdoor Training Model Established

Decided to specialise in outdoor park training. Built a reputation for results-driven, energetic sessions. Word of mouth began to grow the business rapidly.

📈
2019–2020
100+ Clients & Expansion

Hit 100 clients trained. Expanded into Hackney, Chingford, and Ilford. Survived the pandemic by pivoting to online coaching — and discovered the power of remote accountability.

🏆
2021–2023
350+ Clients & 5-Star Reputation

Earned consistent 5-star Google reviews. Hit a 350+ client milestone. Developed structured transformation programmes and introduced nutrition coaching as a core service.

🚀
2024–Present
Over a Decade of Results

Now operating across 12+ East London locations. Clients across East London have completed programmes. Still training outdoors. Still obsessed with results. Still getting better every year.
