Train, Play, Repeat: Your Ultimate Guide to Outdoor Fitness & Recreation

By Dean Burt · March 2024 · 5 min read

This final part of the East London parks series covers three brilliant spaces — each suited to a different kind of training. Whether you want a full outdoor gym, calisthenics bars, or riverside endurance running, these parks deliver.

Lloyd Park Walthamstow: Training & Facilities

Premium Outdoor Exercise & Community Amenities

  • A modern, well-maintained outdoor gym equipped with essential resistance machines for full-body strength training.
  • Professional tennis courts and lawn bowling greens that are easily bookable through the Waltham Forest council website.
  • Expansive green spaces perfectly suited for high-intensity circuit training, sports drills, and large group fitness classes.
  • A state-of-the-art children's playground designed for inclusive play, allowing parents to stay active while kids explore.
  • Clean and accessible public toilet facilities that are consistently maintained throughout the park's opening hours.
  • Peaceful landscaped gardens providing the perfect natural backdrop for cool-down routines, yoga, or a healthy picnic.
  • An on-site café offering a variety of fresh refreshments, artisan coffee, and healthy post-workout snack options.

Victory Park: Community Fitness & Calisthenics

Local Training Spots for Bodyweight & Running

  • Specialized outdoor fitness equipment featuring pull-up and dip bars, perfect for calisthenics and upper-body conditioning.
  • Flat, versatile open areas that serve as the ideal foundation for bodyweight exercises, agility drills, and personal training.
  • A dedicated small-sided football court perfect for practicing footwork, shooting skills, or a casual game with friends.
  • A classic children's playground featuring swings and slides, providing a safe space for local families to enjoy.
  • A lack of permanent on-site toilet facilities means users should plan their training sessions accordingly.
  • Strategic picnic spots and wooden benches positioned around the park for resting between sets or enjoying the outdoors.
  • A network of well-maintained pathways that provide a smooth surface for running loops, sprints, and interval training.

Pro Tip: Victory Park for Calisthenics Beginners

Victory Park's pull-up and dip bars are some of the best-positioned I've found in East London — great sightlines for coaching form. I bring clients here specifically for bodyweight progressions. The flat open areas alongside mean you can pair bar work with ground-based circuits without moving location.

Barking Riverside: Little Bee Park Training

Modern Riverside Fitness & Endurance Running

  • Expansive open-plan areas that offer plenty of room for high-intensity interval training (HIIT) and group bodyweight circuits.
  • A creatively designed, bee-themed children's playground equipped with engaging climbing frames and interactive elements.
  • The park lacks on-site toilet facilities, so it is best suited for shorter, high-impact training sessions.
  • Designated picnic areas with sturdy wooden benches, offering a place to recharge after a riverside workout.
  • Excellent running connectivity, with paths leading directly onto the Thames Path for those seeking long-distance endurance training.
  • As the park has limited natural shade, we recommend planning your outdoor workouts for the cooler morning or evening hours during summer.
