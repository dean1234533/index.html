How This Guide Works

The Rules
Are Simple

Fat loss comes down to one thing: a sustained calorie deficit, fuelled by enough protein to protect your muscle. Everything else — timing, supplements, fancy equipment — is detail.

This guide gives you 7 days of structured workouts and one nutrition principle per day. Follow it, feel the shift, and message Dean when you want to take it further.

Nutrition
Calorie Deficit

Aim for 300–500 kcal below your maintenance. Use an app like MyFitnessPal for the first week to calibrate.

Protein
1.6–2g per kg bodyweight

This protects muscle while you lose fat. Chicken, eggs, Greek yoghurt, fish — keep it simple.

Hydration
2–3 litres daily

Dehydration mimics hunger. Drink a full glass first thing every morning before anything else.

Sleep
7–9 hours non-negotiable

Sleep deprivation raises ghrelin (hunger hormone). Poor sleep kills fat loss faster than a bad meal.

Sunday · Day 7 of 7

Rest
& Reset

Recovery

You've earned this. Rest is not optional — it's when your body actually changes. Today is about recovery, reflection, and preparation for whatever comes next.

Gentle Movement

A 20–30 min walk is perfect. Don't sit still all day, but don't push either. Movement aids recovery without adding stress.

Reflect on the Week

What sessions felt strong? Where did nutrition slip? No judgement — just awareness. That's the data for next week.

Sleep Priority

Go to bed 30 minutes earlier tonight. Your body does its best repair work in deep sleep — don't shortchange it.

What's Next

One week changes your habits. Four weeks changes your body. Twelve weeks changes your life. Book a call with Dean.

Nutrition tip

Stay hydrated and don't skip meals to compensate for the weekend. Undereating on rest days leads to overeating tomorrow. Keep protein high and eat at regular intervals.
