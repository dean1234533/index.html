BEFORE YOUR FIRST ROUND

A few things
you might ask.

Still have a question? Ask Dean on WhatsApp ↗

Do you offer boxing fitness in East London?+

Yes. DB's Workouts offers 1-to-1 boxing fitness with outdoor padwork and conditioning across East London. Book a free consultation with Dean to discuss your goals and location.

Can beginners book boxing fitness sessions?+

Yes. Dean adapts padwork and conditioning to your starting fitness level and experience. You do not need previous boxing fitness experience.

Is this boxing fitness or competitive boxing coaching?+

These sessions focus on fitness, padwork and conditioning. If you want sparring or competitive boxing preparation, discuss that separately with Dean before booking.

Do I need to bring boxing equipment?+

Dean provides the training equipment. Wear comfortable training clothes and suitable footwear, and confirm any personal equipment requirements during your consultation.

How much does boxing fitness cost?+

A 1-to-1 session costs £50 pay as you go. Blocks cost £237.50 for 5, £450 for 10 or £637.50 for 15 sessions. Arrange the boxing fitness focus with Dean when booking.
