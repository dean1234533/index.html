Quick answer

DB's Workouts offers online strength training coaching: 45-minute 1-to-1 sessions with Dean Burt for £30, a personalised progressive workout plan and nutrition plan in the DB's Workouts app, and regular check-ins to adjust your training.

Strength training, coached one-to-one

Good strength training is about doing the right exercises, with good technique, in a sensible order. In your sessions Dean talks you through the movements and coaches how you perform them, whether you train with a barbell, dumbbells, bands or just your bodyweight.

A plan that progresses with you

Strength comes from gradually asking a little more of your body over time, by adding weight, repetitions or difficulty when you are ready. Your workout plan in the DB's Workouts app is built that way, and your check-ins give Dean the information to change it when something is too easy, too hard or stalling.

Technique first

Moving well comes before lifting heavy. Dean starts at a level that suits you, so your technique is solid before the load increases.

At home or in the gym

You can follow your plan at home with minimal kit or in a gym if you have one. Tell Dean what you have access to and he plans around it. See online training at home.

Fuel for your training

Your plan includes a personalised nutrition plan to support your training. Nutrition guidance from a personal trainer is general guidance, not medical or dietetic advice.

Your questions answered

Can I build strength with online personal training?+

Yes. Dean plans progressive strength training around your goals and the equipment you have, and adjusts it at your regular check-ins.

Do I need weights at home?+

Not to begin. Dean can start with bodyweight and bands, and you can add dumbbells, a kettlebell or gym access later.

Will Dean check my technique?+

Yes. Your 45-minute sessions are one-to-one, and Dean coaches how you perform each movement.

How much does online strength coaching cost?+

£30 per 45-minute session, including your workout and nutrition plan in the app and regular check-ins.

Is it suitable for beginners?+

Yes. Beginners are welcome, and the training starts at a level that suits you. See online training for beginners.
