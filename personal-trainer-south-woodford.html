Frequently Asked Questions: Personal Training in South Woodford

Is there an outdoor personal trainer in South Woodford?+

Yes. DB's Workouts, run by Dean Burt (REPs Level 3 and Active IQ Level 3 qualified), offers outdoor 1-to-1 personal training in South Woodford (E18). It is rated 5.0 stars on Google from 70 reviews.

Where do personal training sessions in South Woodford take place?+

Sessions in South Woodford take place in local green spaces including Wanstead Flats, George Green, South Woodford, Epping Forest (Roding Valley) and Snaresbrook Crown Court Green. Dean can also train you in your own home garden.

How much does a personal trainer cost in South Woodford?+

A single session is £45 (pay as you go). Blocks are cheaper per session: 5 sessions for £214, 10 sessions for £406 or 15 sessions for £576 (£38.40 per session). Pair sessions are for two people and cost 1.5 times the corresponding one-person price. Pay as you go is £67.50 total for both people per session. NHS and key workers get 14% off blocks and students get 10% off 10 and 15 session blocks.

Do I get a nutrition plan and workout plan with personal training in South Woodford?+

Yes, free with every session block (5, 10 or 15 sessions). Dean sets you up in the DB's Workouts app and emails you a personal link, so you get a personalised nutrition plan and workout plan to follow between sessions. Pay-as-you-go sessions don't include the app plans. The app is also available separately to anyone from £7.99 a month at app.dbworkouts.co.uk.

Can I train online instead of in South Woodford?+

Yes. DB's Workouts also offers online 1-to-1 personal training for £30 per 45-minute session, from wherever you are. You get a personalised workout and nutrition plan in the DB's Workouts app and regular check-ins. See online personal training.

Do I need a gym membership or my own equipment?+

No. Sessions are outdoors and Dean brings the equipment. You only need comfortable training clothes and suitable footwear.

Can complete beginners book outdoor personal training?+

Yes. Every new client starts with a free consultation and an assessment, and Dean builds the programme around your fitness level and goals. There is no contract or minimum commitment.
